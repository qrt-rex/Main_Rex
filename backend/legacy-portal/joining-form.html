Please read the company policies below carefully. Digital sign-off is mandatory for official employee enrollment.

1. Purpose

This document establishes the official employment standards, responsibilities, and terms governing your engagement with Rexera Technologies Inc. (the "Company").

2. Employment Terms

Your employment is subject to satisfactory verification of educational credentials, previous employment references, background checks, and identity proofs.

3. Roles and Responsibilities

You agree to perform all duties assigned to you with the highest standard of professional integrity, dedication, and diligence.

4. Compensation and Benefits

Your monthly salary will be calculated and disbursed as per your agreed offer letter, inclusive of statutory deductions like Provident Fund (PF) and Professional Tax (PT).

5. Work Hours & Remote Standards

The standard working hours are 9 hours per day (including meal breaks), Monday through Friday, with flexibility as determined by project and operational requirements.

6. Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to annual earned leaves, sick leaves, and statutory public holidays as detailed in the official Rexera Leave Guidelines.

7. Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure

You shall strictly maintain the absolute confidentiality of all proprietary code, client data, business strategies, and intellectual property of Rexera both during and after your employment.

8. Code of Conduct

Rexera maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against discrimination, harassment, unethical conduct, or actions detrimental to company reputation.

9. Disciplinary Action

Violations of company policy may result in disciplinary proceedings up to and including immediate termination and legal recourse.

10. Performance Reviews

Formal performance appraisals and reviews are conducted bi-annually to evaluate goals, achievements, and career progression.

11. Termination of Employment

Either party may terminate employment by serving the designated notice period or salary in lieu thereof, as defined in your offer contract.

12. Digital Acknowledgment & Consent

By submitting this form and ticking the declaration below, you confirm that you have read, understood, and voluntarily agree to abide by all the terms herein.

13. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

This agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India, under the jurisdiction of the courts of Hyderabad, Telangana.
